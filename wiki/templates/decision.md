# Decision record template

Copy into `wiki/decisions/` with the next number, actual date, cited source IDs, and a descriptive title. Link it in the index and log.

**Decision:** describe the concrete choice.

**Context and evidence:** link measurements and sources; label untested assumptions.

**Alternatives considered:** explain relevant tradeoffs without claiming an alternative is impossible unless supported.

**Consequences:** implementation work, limitations, and validation required.

**Revisit when:** define a measurable trigger or a new piece of evidence.
