**Reading scope:** OpenSSH ssh(1) manual: description and -L, -R, -N, -T and connection-sharing options.

## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** SSH can carry arbitrary TCP forwarding. An explicit loopback bind keeps a forwarded service local to the machine initiating the tunnel.

**Limitations:** A tunnel does not solve unreachable SSH endpoints by itself, preserve optimizer state, or provide exactly-once aggregation. Commands in synthesis are proposed connection templates, not executed sessions. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
