## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** FedEx-LoRA keeps factor averages and adds their product-aggregation residual to frozen weights. The paper describes low-rank communication of residuals.

**Limitations:** Applying residuals to a frozen 4-bit base requires extra numerical/state design; exact aggregation is not exact centralized optimization. Not a drop-in claim for MacReduce. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
