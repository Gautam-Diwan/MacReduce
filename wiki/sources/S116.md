Address translation and the DART The engine reads and writes DRAM through its own IOMMU, the DART. It translates a device address into a physical page at a 16 KB granule across a 3.5 GiB aperture. A normal engine page is one 64-bit leaf word, the physical frame located unshifted with bit 63 set: leaf=phys∣0x8000000000000000.
