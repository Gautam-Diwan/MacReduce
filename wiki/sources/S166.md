## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** Tailscale offers direct UDP, peer relay and DERP paths with end-to-end WireGuard encryption. Direct paths generally give better latency/throughput; a path may change during a run.

**Limitations:** No guaranteed direct reachability or measured campus/home throughput. Recovered from a failed obsolete /docs/concepts/connection-types URL using /docs/reference/connection-types. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
