## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** Unequal local work can change the effective objective under naive averaging. Solver-specific normalization matters; the scalar divide-by-step-count expression is the plain-SGD special case.

**Limitations:** No validation here of that scalar correction for AdamW, persistent adaptive moments, or nonlinear LoRA factorization. Supplements the earlier S06 abstract and equation reading. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
