## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** Listed bundles include USD 7/month for 1 GB and USD 12/month for 2 GB, with disk and transfer allowances. Both inbound and outbound usage count against the bundled allowance.

**Limitations:** These are virtual servers, not Apple training hardware. Regional allowances differ; bundled-transfer limits and overage still matter. Superseded narrative examples elsewhere on the page were not used for current bundle rates. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
