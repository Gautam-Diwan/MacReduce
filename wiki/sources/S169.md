**Reading scope:** Complete AWS Markdown: hardware, dedicated-host constraints, allocation, availability, readiness and storage.

## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** EC2 Mac offers Apple Silicon hardware, one instance per dedicated host, a 24-hour minimum allocation, and no Spot/Reserved instance option. AWS reports launch readiness around 6–20 minutes.

**Limitations:** API/ANEForge access and capacity are untested. M4 Mac mini 24 GiB and M1 Mac mini 16 GiB do not reproduce the team laptops. This reading is newer than existing hardware assumptions. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
