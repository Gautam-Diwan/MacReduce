## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** FedAvg alternates parallel local work and weighted parameter aggregation from a common broadcast. Data weights are distinct from hardware speed. Local work trades communication rounds against optimization behavior.

**Limitations:** Original SGD experiments do not establish AdamW equivalence, Qwen3 quality, or wireless Mac speedup. Supplements the abstract-only S20 card; does not replace its snapshot. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
