Telemetry and hardware counters The engine has a hardware performance-counter block of twenty-four per-task-descriptor counters, master enable at ANEProgramCreateArgs+0x6c, and free-running firmware timestamp at MMIO 0x2_6b17_8000. The block geometry and the timestamp are readable; the per-task-descriptor counter values are not.
