**Original URL:** [primary source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111883)

**Reading scope:** Apple M1 Air specifications: chip, memory and wireless sections.

## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** Apple lists a 16-core Neural Engine, 8 GB configuration and Wi-Fi 6 on the M1 Air, with 7- or 8-core GPU variants.

**Limitations:** Specifications do not establish ANEForge compatibility, 4-bit fine-tuning memory headroom, or sustained GPU/network throughput on the user machine. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
