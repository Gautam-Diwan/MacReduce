## Findings

- Data-parallel training in MLX is one added line: `grads = mx.nn.average_gradients(grads)`, which batches several gradients into fewer communications than a per-tensor `all_sum`. The page recommends this over per-gradient reduction because many small communications hurt performance ([S02](S02.md) repeats this under "Tips and Tricks").
- Nothing on the page addresses heterogeneous workers, unequal local steps, or Wi-Fi. It is synchronous gradient averaging each step.

## What this source blocks

- A plain data-parallel trainer over MLX is first-party. MacReduce's existing coordinator/worker trainer offers infrequent averaging (local SGD) instead of per-step all-reduce; that schedule is the subject of [S04](S04.md)–[S08](S08.md) and is not new either. [S28](S28.md) shows people already run `mlx_lm lora` data-parallel across Macs with this primitive.
