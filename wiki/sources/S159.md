## Findings and limitations

A linear regression trained with `af.parameter`, `af.mse`, and `af.Trainer(obj, [w], lr=0.05, optimizer='adam', device_optimizer=True)`. The host writes `tr.data[x]` and `tr.data[target]` each step and calls `tr.step()`. Forward, backward, and Adam are the trainer's on-engine step. This is the small-model pattern, not a Qwen block.
