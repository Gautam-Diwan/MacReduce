## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** AWS describes a shared monthly Internet-egress allowance across services/regions, with exceptions. An external coordinator sends a global adapter to every Mac, so downloads are billable egress once allowances are exhausted.

**Limitations:** Did not verify a current Linux or CUDA instance hourly quote from this page. No t4g or g5 rate is presented as verified in the synthesis. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
