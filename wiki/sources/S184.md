## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** DiLoCo is FedAvg with many inner steps, AdamW inside, and SGD with Nesterov momentum as the outer optimizer applied to the averaged parameter delta (the "outer gradient"). With outer SGD at learning rate 1 it reduces to FedAvg. Outer learning rate 0.7 with momentum 0.9 was reported robust and used throughout; plain SGD (FedAvg) and Adam outer optimizers performed worse. Varying H from 50 to 2000 on a 150M model, more frequent communication helped but gains were small below H = 500, and H = 1000 cost 2.9% perplexity against H = 50 while communicating 20 times less. A cosine outer learning-rate decay gave similar results because the inner schedule already shrinks outer gradients. Dropping each replica's communication with 50% probability raised perplexity by 2.1% in the non-i.i.d. setting. A single replica with H = 500 and the outer Nesterov step also improved over the baseline.

**Limitations:** all results are full-parameter pretraining or continued pretraining of Chinchilla-style models on C4 with 8 replicas and data-center accelerators. Nothing here covers LoRA adapters, a 4-bit frozen base, AdamW moments persisted across rounds on three heterogeneous Macs, or Wi-Fi transport. The outer hyperparameters were tuned for that setting and need re-tuning for MacReduce. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
