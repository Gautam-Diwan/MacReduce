**Reading scope:** abstract and metadata only. Repository documentation is not a code audit; source-reported measurements have not been reproduced by MacReduce.

FusionML studies Apple CPU/GPU co-execution and reports workload-dependent results: prefill gains, but no general matched-precision training or decode benefit in its evaluated cases. It highlights lazy execution, synchronization, and calibration effects. Treat these as reported results, not our measurements or a universal impossibility result. Full-paper and code review remain necessary.
