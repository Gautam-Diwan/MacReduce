Cross-silicon targets The compiler builds 28 architecture targets, one per silicon profile, under the fixed relation M(n)→H(n+12). A suffix letter selects the NE-core count, and the operation surface stops expanding at A15, so the surface measured on the M5 is the surface for everything above it.
