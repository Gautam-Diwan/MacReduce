## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** Separate averaging of LoRA factors differs from averaging their matrix products. Stacking factors with one application of each client weight represents the product average exactly and supports unequal ranks.

**Limitations:** Stacked adapter size and downstream state handling differ from fixed-rank factor FedAvg. No Mac, ANE, or quantized-base reproduction. Method review only, no novelty assessment. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
