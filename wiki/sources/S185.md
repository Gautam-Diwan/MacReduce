## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** Streaming DiLoCo synchronizes one fragment of transformer blocks at a time on a fixed offset schedule, so each fragment still sees H inner steps between syncs while peak bandwidth falls by fragment size over depth (8 times for 3-layer fragments of 24). Overlap lets workers keep training for tau inner steps before the fragment's merged update arrives, mixed with a factor alpha; degradation was under 0.2% up to tau = 10, and a per-worker tau up to 5 tolerated heterogeneous worker speeds. Outer gradients were sent as 4-bit floats (E3M0) while accumulation stayed FP32, with no observed regression. In the compression ablation, lowering precision to FP4 did not affect loss or HellaSwag, whereas zeroing values (FedDropout, DARE, top-k) was significantly worse. The main runs used H = 100 and outer learning rate 0.4 tuned at small scale.

**Limitations:** pretraining from scratch on C4 and Dolma with data-center accelerators and full-parameter outer gradients. No error feedback is described for the FP4 path; whether LoRA deltas from a few inner steps on a 4-bit base tolerate FP4 equally well is untested. Overlap and fragment scheduling assume a custom collective, not Flower's synchronous fit round. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
