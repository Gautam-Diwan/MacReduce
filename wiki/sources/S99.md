Across the chip family An M(n) chip has the H(n+12) ANE architecture, so M1 is H13 and M5 is H17. A network that compiles and runs on one generation does so on the others. One compiler binary builds every target, and only a per-target data table changes. Only fp16 numerics vary from chip to chip, and only at a unit in the last place.
