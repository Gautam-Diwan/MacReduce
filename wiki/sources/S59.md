## What this page is for

The optimizer and the cost model¶ ANEForge lowers a graph with two cooperating pieces: an optimizer/tuner that chooses among proven-equivalent lowerings, and an analytic cost model that orders those choices so the tuner can prune what it measures. The optimizer and opt levels¶ compile(out, opt=...) selects the graph optimizer, separating lossless route selection from lossy variants, and on-device measurement from measurement-free cost estimation.
