## What this page is for

On-ANE autograd¶ ANEForge implements a small reverse-mode autograd over its own graph IR in which forward, backward, and the optimizer update all compile to ANE programs. The host never runs tensor math during a training step - it only samples minibatches, computes a scalar learning rate, shuttles state, and prints. This page documents how that works and the ANE/numerics reasons behind the non-obvious choices. Core model¶
