**Reading scope:** Markdown overview, requirements, UDP relay setup, grant-policy examples and connection diagnostics.

## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** An operator-managed peer relay can be used when direct connectivity fails, ahead of DERP. It requires a reachable relay port and policy configuration.

**Limitations:** A peer relay adds a traffic hop and is distinct from running an application coordinator or an exit node. No relay throughput measured. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
