## Findings and limitations

**Limitations:** Memory-saving options do not prove that this exact workload fits the 8 GB M1. Reducing layers changes the adapter study. Supplements unpinned S19; the project keeps its own recipe. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
