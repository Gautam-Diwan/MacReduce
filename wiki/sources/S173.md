**Reading scope:** HTML text extraction: public IPv4 and NAT gateway prices/examples.

## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** AWS lists public IPv4 at USD 0.005/address-hour. Its NAT example charges USD 0.045/hour per AZ and USD 0.045/GB processing, in addition to applicable transfer.

**Limitations:** NAT example is region-specific. A simple public SSH coordinator does not require a managed NAT gateway. No deployed architecture or actual bill inspected. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
