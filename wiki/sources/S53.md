## What this page is for

Native bridges and segmentation¶ Some ANE hardware layers exist on the silicon but Apple's user-space MIL/CoreML compiler never emits them. ANEForge reaches them by hand-authoring native ANECIR netplists and dispatching them directly (Path A) - each such op cuts the graph and runs as a separate native sub-program.
