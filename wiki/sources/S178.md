## Findings and limitations

**Source-reported:** Flower distinguishes transport encryption from node authentication, and documents certificates for its deployment connections.

**Limitations:** Server-authenticated TLS is not mutual TLS. Pin the chosen framework version and configure client authentication separately. No Flower installation or deployment tested. No external benchmark performance has been reproduced by this reading.
