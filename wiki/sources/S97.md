Power and efficiency The engine draws less power than the GPU on every workload class measured, including the ones where the GPU is faster. On the M1 it delivers 2 to 14 times the GPU energy efficiency. The advantage holds across the M1, M2, and M5, so it is a property of the fixed-function fp16 datapath, not of one chip.
